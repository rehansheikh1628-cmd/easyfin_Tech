Scanned & Image PDFs (OCR Guidance)

Digital Text Required

ACCUFEX is engineered around a deterministic coordinate-based digital parser. It extracts characters and bounding boxes directly from the PDF's native font glyphs.

Important Notice: Scanned statements, photocopies, passbook camera snapshots, and image-only PDFs require Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and are not currently included in the standard digital-PDF parser workflow.
Quick test: Open your bank PDF in a viewer. If you can click and highlight transaction text with your mouse cursor, it is a digital PDF compatible with ACCUFEX.

Format Variation & Layout Matching

Dynamic Calibration

Bank support depends strictly on statement layout. A single bank may generate different layouts across retail netbanking, corporate portals, passbook counters, or credit card divisions.

  • Deterministic Scoring: Upon upload, ACCUFEX's bank detector scans IFSC codes, branch patterns, and column header configurations to match the correct parser profile.
  • Mathematical Validation: Every extracted row is reconciled against the running balance. If any column is misaligned, ACCUFEX flags it in the Review screen rather than guessing.
