Frequently Asked Questions

Is any payment required right now?

No. EasyFin Tech is in Phase 1 Foundation mode. All preview interfaces and conversion workflows are free for evaluation.

When will final pricing be established?

Commercial pricing will be finalized upon the rollout of the automated Phase 2 extraction engine.

Can I test password-protected statements?

Yes, the converter UI is designed to accept statement unlock credentials during ingestion.

What accounting tools can import the Excel file?

Any spreadsheet software (Excel, LibreOffice, Google Sheets) or accounting package that imports `.xlsx` or `.csv` (Tally, QuickBooks, Zoho Books, SAP).
